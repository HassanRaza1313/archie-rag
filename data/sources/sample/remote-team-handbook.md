# Remote Team Handbook

## Working hours

The team is distributed across time zones, so we protect a shared core window from 14:00 to 17:00 UTC for
meetings and quick decisions. Outside the core window, people organise their own hours. Status in the chat tool
should show when you are away.

## Communication

Default to written, asynchronous communication. Decisions that affect more than one team are written up as a
short proposal with the problem, the options considered and the recommendation, and are open for comments for
at least two working days. Urgent production issues go to the on-call channel, never to direct messages.

| Situation | Channel | Expected response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Production incident | On-call channel | 15 minutes |
| Blocking question | Team channel | Same working day |
| Proposal review | Document comments | Two working days |
| Social chat | Watercooler channel | None |

## Meetings

Every meeting needs an agenda shared at least a day before, and notes with decisions and owners shared within
an hour after. If a meeting has no agenda, any attendee may decline it. Recurring meetings are reviewed every
quarter and cancelled if they no longer earn their time.

## Time off

Employees receive 25 days of paid leave per year plus local public holidays. Request leave in the HR system at
least two weeks ahead for absences longer than three days. Unused leave of up to five days carries over into
the first quarter of the next year.

## Equipment

New starters receive a laptop and a home-office budget of 600 euros for a desk, chair or monitor. Equipment
remains company property; return it within two weeks of leaving.
